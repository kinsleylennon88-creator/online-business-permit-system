Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take to process a business permit?

A: Our target processing time is 2-3 business days for complete applications with all required documents. You can track your application status in real-time through your dashboard.

Q: What documents do I need for a new business permit?

A: You need: Zoning Clearance, Occupancy Permit, Barangay Clearance, Cedula, Police Clearance, DTI/SEC Registration, and Sanitary Permit. All documents need 1 photocopy each.

Q: Can I pay the permit fees online?

A: Yes! We accept payments through GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfer. You can also pay at the Municipal Treasurer's Office during business hours.

Q: Do I need to visit the municipal hall to apply?

A: No, our online system allows you to complete the entire application process digitally. However, you'll need to obtain some supporting documents (like Zoning Clearance) from respective offices.
