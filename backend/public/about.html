About Janiuay BPLO

The Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO) of the Municipality of Janiuay is dedicated to providing efficient, transparent, and business-friendly services to entrepreneurs and business owners in our community.

Our Mission

To streamline business permit processing through digital innovation, ensuring fast, transparent, and hassle-free services that promote economic growth and support local entrepreneurship in Janiuay.

Our Vision

To become a model LGU business licensing office in the Philippines, recognized for excellence in public service, innovation, and support for MSMEs and large enterprises alike.

Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, efficiency, and customer service while continuously improving our processes to better serve the business community of Janiuay.

Our Services

New Business Permit

Application and issuance of Mayor's Permit for new businesses operating in Janiuay.

Renewal

Annual renewal of business permits with streamlined document requirements.

Amendments

Updates to business information such as address, ownership, or business activity changes.

Consultation

Free consultation on business requirements, zoning, and compliance matters.
